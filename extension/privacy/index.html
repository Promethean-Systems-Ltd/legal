Maestro Studio extension

Privacy policy · Promethean Systems Ltd · Last updated 19 November 2026

The Maestro Studio browser extension lets someone who works with Maestro Studio bring the web page they are on into their studio's Maestro, ask Maestro about it, and fill it in after approving each step. This page explains what it reads, where that goes, and what it never does.

When it reads anything

Only on the tab where you click the Maestro button in the toolbar, and only when you then press a button in its panel ("Bring it in", "Ask" or "Show me what Maestro would do"). It has no access to other tabs or sites, and does nothing in the background.

What it reads, and where it goes

  • The page's title and address, its tables, its text, and the labels and current values of its form fields.
  • That is sent only to your own studio's Maestro, the address you paired the extension with, over HTTPS. It is not sent to Promethean Systems or anyone else by the extension.
  • In Maestro, it is handled like anything else in your studio: files go to the studio's Drive, and AI answers use the studio's own Maestro set-up. Only people with access to that studio can see it.

What it never does

  • It never reads password fields or card details, and never types them.
  • It never clicks anything that sends, submits, saves or pays unless you tick that step yourself.
  • It does not track your browsing, collect your history, use cookies of its own, show ads, or sell or share data.

What it keeps on your device

Your Maestro's address and a pairing token, in the browser's extension storage, so you stay paired. Unpairing (in the extension or in Maestro under Settings → Browser extension) deletes the token; removing the extension deletes both.

Your data in Maestro

What is brought into Maestro is kept, changed and deleted under your studio's agreement with Maestro and its privacy notice. Ask your studio's Maestro partner or the Maestro team to see, export or delete it.
